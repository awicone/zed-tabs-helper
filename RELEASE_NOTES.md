A small macOS helper for Zed's system-window-tabs workaround: restore project windows, toggle tabs, and merge them after launch. It also prepares settings before a normal quit.

Download **Zed-Tabs-Helper-0.1.0-universal.zip**, unzip it, and move the app to Applications. Open the included **READ ME.md** for Accessibility permission and login startup instructions. No build tools are required.

- Universal binary for Apple Silicon and Intel, built for macOS 13+.
- Ad-hoc signed, **not Apple-notarized**. macOS may require Open Anyway in Privacy & Security.
- Requires stable Zed with English menus and Accessibility access.
- The original flow was tested on Apple Silicon with Zed 1.22.0; Intel and older macOS versions have not been tested interactively.
- Accessibility permission may need to be removed and re-added after updates.

This is an unofficial workaround for zed-industries/zed#49724 and #44042. See the README for settings changed and known limitations. SHA-256 checksums are included with the download.
